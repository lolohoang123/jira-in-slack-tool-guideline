Support

We're happy to help. No account is needed: just send us an e-mail.

Contact support

support@YOUR-DOMAIN

We reply within 2 business days.

What to include

  • Your Slack workspace name.
  • What you typed or clicked (for example /jira-agent list or the Approve button).
  • The message you saw, and roughly when it happened (with your time zone).
Never send passwords, tokens or screenshots that show them. We will never ask for them.

Quick answers

Most problems are solved in a minute. In Slack, type /jira-agent help to see every command.

You seeWhat to do
“This Slack workspace hasn't installed the Jira app yet” Install the app from the home page, or ask your Slack admin to approve it.
“Your Jira account is not connected” Type /jira-agent connect and approve access on the Atlassian page.
“This link has expired or has already been used” Links work once, for 10 minutes. Type /jira-agent connect again for a new one.
“Your Jira authorization has expired or was revoked” Type /jira-agent connect to connect again.
“I couldn't send you the approval … as a direct message” Open Jira Agent under Apps in Slack's sidebar so the conversation exists, then log the time again.
Times or “today” look wrong Check the time zone in your Atlassian profile (Account settings).

More answers, with pictures, are in the user guide.

Uninstall and delete your data

  • Disconnect your Jira account: type /jira-agent disconnect. Your stored Jira tokens are deleted.
  • Revoke Jira access: Atlassian account settings → Connected apps → remove Jira Agent.
  • Uninstall from the workspace (Slack admin): Slack → Apps → Jira Agent → Configuration → Remove App. All data stored for that workspace is deleted automatically.
  • Anything else, such as a copy or deletion of your worklog request history: e-mail us. See the Privacy Policy for what we store and for how long.

Security issues

If you think you found a security problem, e-mail support@YOUR-DOMAIN with the subject “Security”. Please do not post it publicly until it is fixed.
