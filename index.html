The problem it solves

Logging time in Jira means leaving the conversation, finding the ticket, opening the worklog dialog and remembering what you did. Jira Agent brings that into Slack, where the work is discussed: find the ticket, enter the duration, approve, done.

How it works

  1. Install the app in your Slack workspace with the Add to Slack button. You land on a page that confirms the installation and tells you what to do next.
  2. Connect Jira once: type /jira-agent connect in Slack and approve access on the Atlassian page (the same kind of screen as “Sign in with Google”). If your account has several Jira sites, you choose one.
  3. Find a ticket with /jira-agent list or /jira-agent logwork find <keyword>, click Log Time, enter the duration.
  4. Approve the request the app sends you as a direct message. Only then is the worklog written to Jira, under your own Jira account.

Step-by-step screens are in the user guide.

What you can do

📋
See your tickets

Tickets assigned to you, their status and the total time already logged.

🔍
Find any ticket

By keyword or by key such as PROJ-123.

🕒
Today's time

What you logged today, with the total, in your own time zone.

✍️
Log work with approval

A short form, then an Approve / Reject message. Duplicate requests are blocked.

Safe by design

✅
You approve every write

Only the person who made a request can approve it, and only once. Requests expire after 24 hours.

🔒
Encrypted tokens

Slack and Jira tokens are encrypted with AWS KMS and are never written to logs.

💬
No access to your messages

The app cannot read channels, messages or files. Replies are visible only to you.

Slack permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy the app needs it
commandsTo add the /jira-agent command.
chat:writeTo send you the worklog approval message and its result.
users:readTo read your Slack time zone, only when your Jira profile has none, so “today” and start times are correct.

On the Jira side the app asks to read your profile, read tickets and worklogs, and add worklogs (read:jira-user, read:jira-work, write:jira-work, offline_access).

Requirements

  • A Slack workspace where you may install apps (some companies require an admin to approve new apps).
  • A Jira Cloud account (yourcompany.atlassian.net). Jira Server and Data Center are not supported.
