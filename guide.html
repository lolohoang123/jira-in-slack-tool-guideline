User guide

Log your Jira time right inside Slack

Jira Agent lets you see your tickets and log work time to Jira without leaving Slack. All you need is a short command that starts with /jira-agent.

🕒

✍️

Safe by design: Jira Agent never writes to Jira on its own. Every time you log work you get a confirmation message, and nothing is saved until you click Approve.

2Before you start

You need two things:

  • Jira Agent installed in your company's Slack.
    A Slack admin usually does this once for the whole company. If you type a command and see “This Slack workspace hasn't installed the Jira app yet”, click the Install the app link in that message, or ask your admin to install it.
  • A Jira (Atlassian) account that you already use every day, for example yourcompany.atlassian.net.
Tip: In channels, every Jira Agent reply is marked “Only visible to you”, so you can type commands in any channel without disturbing anyone.

3Connect your Jira account (one time only)

This step lets Jira Agent read your tickets and log time under your name. It works just like clicking “Sign in with Google” on a website.

Step 1 — Type the connect command

In the message box of any channel, type /jira-agent connect and press Enter.

Step 2 — Click “Connect Jira” and allow access

Your browser opens an Atlassian page. If you are not signed in yet, sign in to your Jira account. Then:

  1. In “Use app on”, pick your company's Jira site (if you only have one, it is already selected).
  2. Click Accept.

Step 3 — Go back to Slack

When you see “Jira connected successfully”, you're done. You can close the tab and return to Slack.

Note: The Connect Jira link works only once and expires after 10 minutes. If you see “This link has expired or has already been used”, just type /jira-agent connect again to get a new link.

4See your tickets

Type /jira-agent list. After a few seconds you'll see up to 20 tickets assigned to you, most recently updated first.

5Find a ticket

If the ticket isn't assigned to you, or you don't remember its key, type:

/jira-agent logwork find keyword

You typeJira Agent finds
/jira-agent logwork find login bugTickets containing words that start with “login” and “bug”
/jira-agent logwork find PROJ-123Ticket PROJ-123 itself (shown first)

Results look like Figure 4, with a Log Time button on each ticket. If there are more than 20 results, add another keyword to narrow them down.

6See what you logged today

Type /jira-agent logwork today to see what you logged today and your total time.

Time zone: “Today” and start times follow the time zone in your Jira profile. If times look off, check your time zone under Atlassian → Profile → Account settings.

7Log your work time

Three steps: open the form → fill it in → approve.

Step 1 — Open the Log Time form

Click the Log Time button next to a ticket in the results of list, logwork find or logwork today (Figures 4 and 5). The ticket key is filled in for you.

Step 2 — Fill in the form and click “Log Time”

FieldWhat to enter
IssueThe ticket key, for example PROJ-12. Filled in for you when you click Log Time from a list.
DateThe day you did the work. Defaults to today.
DurationHow long you worked. See how to write it in the table below.
Start TimeWhen you started. Defaults to 09:00.
DescriptionOptional. A short note about what you did.
Remaining estimate Use existing estimate: keep the remaining time as it is (pick this if you're not sure).
Adjust automatically: Jira subtracts the time you just logged.
Set a new estimate: type a new remaining time in the field below.

How to write the Duration

You writeIt means
30m30 minutes
2h2 hours
1h 30m1 hour 30 minutes
1d1 working day = 8 hours

The duration must be more than 0, and one entry can be at most 24 hours.

Step 3 — Confirm with “Approve”

After you click Log Time, Jira Agent sends you a direct message (DM). Check the details, then click Approve.

Good to know:
  • Until you click Approve, nothing is saved in Jira.
  • A confirmation request expires after 24 hours. If it expires, log the time again.
  • Only the person who created the request can Approve or Reject it.
  • Sending the exact same request twice (same ticket, date, start time and duration) is blocked, so time is never logged twice.

8Disconnect Jira

If you no longer use Jira Agent, or want to switch to another Jira account or site:

  1. Type /jira-agent disconnect. Jira Agent removes your connection.
  2. (Optional) To fully revoke access, go to Atlassian → Account settings → Connected apps and remove Jira Agent.

To connect again, or to switch to another Jira site, just type /jira-agent connect again. The new connection replaces the old one.

9If something goes wrong

Click the message you see to find out what to do.

“This Slack workspace hasn't installed the Jira app yet”

Jira Agent is not installed in your company's Slack yet. Click Install the app in the message, or ask your Slack admin to install it. If your company requires admin approval for apps, wait for the admin to approve it.

“Your Jira account is not connected. Run /jira-agent connect first.”

You haven't connected Jira yet, or your connection was removed. Follow section 3 again.

“This link has expired or has already been used”

The Connect Jira link is older than 10 minutes or was already clicked. Type /jira-agent connect to get a new link, click it once, and finish within 10 minutes.

A page asks me to “Choose your Jira site”

Your Atlassian account has access to more than one Jira site. Click the site you use for work; you're connected right away. The page works once, for 10 minutes. To switch to another site later, type /jira-agent connect again.

“I couldn't send you the approval … as a direct message”

Jira Agent couldn't send you a direct message, so nothing was saved. In Slack, open Jira Agent under Apps in the left sidebar, open the Messages tab to start the conversation, then log the time again. If you can't see a Messages tab, contact your admin.

“This approval is expired, already handled, or does not belong to you.”

The request is older than 24 hours, was already approved or rejected, or belongs to someone else. If needed, log the time again.

“An identical worklog request … is already waiting for approval”

You already sent this exact request. Find the earlier confirmation message in your DM with Jira Agent and click Approve there.

“Duration must look like 30m, 1h, 1h 30m, or 1d.”

The Duration field isn't written correctly. See how to write the Duration.

“Sorry, I don't recognize that command.”

You typed something that isn't one of the available commands. The reply lists them; you can also type /jira-agent help at any time, or see the cheat sheet below.

I typed a command but got no reply

Wait a few seconds and try again. Check that you typed /jira-agent exactly (with the hyphen). If it still doesn't work, contact support and tell them when you tried.
